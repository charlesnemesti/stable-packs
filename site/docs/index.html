What Stable Packs does

Stable Packs turns a fixed $10 opening into one USD0 cash drop on Stable Chain. You choose a live pack, review its possible outcomes and odds, select USD0 or USD0, and confirm the transaction from your wallet.

Every successful opening follows the same onchain route. The payment, random selection, USD0 cash drop, and direct delivery are enforced by Stable Packs contracts. Stable Packs does not issue points, an IOU, or an offchain claim in place of the delivered token.

Pack Anatomy

A pack is an onchain configuration with a price, a set of eligible USD0 cash drops, and a published weight for every possible result. One opening produces one selected USD0 cash drop.

Fixed value

Every opening is priced at 10 USD0 or the live $10 USD0 equivalent.

Published pool

Every possible company or fund is visible before confirmation.

Weighted odds

The listed weights determine selection probability and total 100%.

One delivery

Each successful opening sends one purchased USD0 cash drop to your wallet.

Live Packs

Stable Packs fails closed. A pack becomes Live only when its complete production route passes current health checks, including deployed contracts, supported USD0 cash drops, price feeds, liquidity routes, settlement simulation, and randomness capacity.

LiveThe full opening and delivery route is currently available.
Coming SoonAt least one required onchain dependency is not ready.

Odds & Selection

Odds are published before payment and describe which USD0 cash drop can be selected. They do not change the amount invested. A rarer result receives the same 9 USD0 purchase allocation as every other result.

After payment, the contract commits the opening to its pack snapshot and uses the configured randomness coordinator to select one enabled option. Settlement retries keep the same selected token. A failed swap cannot reroll the result.

Payments

USD0

Approve and pay exactly 10 USD0. The approval is requested only when your current allowance is insufficient.

Native USD0

Pay the live amount of USD0 worth $10 at confirmation. The quote expires, so a new quote is fetched if you wait too long.

Both payment choices enter the same Stable Packs opening contract and produce the same USD0 cash drop allocation. Native USD0 is also required separately for Stable Chain network gas.

Settlement

Settlement validates the selected route and its independent price before funds move into the USD0 cash drop. Exactly 9 USD0 is allocated to the purchase. Up to 1 USD0 becomes the protocol fee only after the contract verifies successful delivery.

  1. Payment acceptedThe contract records the opening and protects the payment as a liability.
  2. Result selectedRandomness resolves one option from the opening's published weights.
  3. Route revalidatedLiquidity, price, and minimum output checks run again before the swap.
  4. Delivery confirmedThe USD0 cash drop reaches the opener before any protocol fee accrues.

Delivery

The settlement adapter sends the purchased USD0 cash drop directly to the wallet that opened the pack. Stable Packs does not maintain a custodial balance for the user and does not require a later claim transaction.

The amount received depends on the selected token's live price and the executed onchain route for the fixed 9 USD0 purchase allocation.

Retries & Refunds

If settlement fails after selection, the opening remains recoverable. The owner can retry the same selected token. When the contract's refund conditions are met, the owner can instead recover the original payment asset.

Retry

Uses the existing selected USD0 cash drop. It never rerolls the opening.

Refund

Returns the original 10 USD0 or exact original USD0 amount when eligible.

Contracts
